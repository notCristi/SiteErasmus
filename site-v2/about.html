Three schools from Romania, France and Denmark work together in one Erasmus+ project. Here you can read what it is about and who makes it happen.

Group photo · all three schools
The idea

Different places. Same questions.

Our schools are far from each other. One is near Paris, one is close to the sea in the north of Denmark, and one is in the old town of Brașov, under the mountains.

But our students ask the same questions. How can young people take part in their school and their town? How do we live well together when we are all different? In this project we look for answers together.
