How this website handles your data, how we use photos of students, and who made what.

Privacy policy

This website does not use cookies, ads or tracking. It does not collect any personal data from visitors.

The fonts, maps, photos and videos are stored on this website. Your browser does not connect to Google or other companies to show them. Only videos that we embed from [Google Drive / YouTube] load from that service.

The website is hosted by [GitHub Pages / Cloudflare]. Like every web host, they may keep short technical logs.

Photo consent

We only publish photos and videos of students who agreed to it. For students under 18, a parent agreed too.

Do you want a photo removed? Write to [email@example.com] and we will take it down.
