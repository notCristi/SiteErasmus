Legal

How this website handles your data, how to ask for a photo to be removed, and who made what.

Privacy policy

This website has no ads, no tracking and no cookies. If you only visit it, we do not collect any personal data about you.

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

The fonts and the design files are stored on this website. There are two exceptions. The maps on the Journey page come from OpenStreetMap, so your browser connects to OpenStreetMap’s servers when you open one of these maps. If a video on this website is embedded from YouTube or Google Drive, it loads from that service.

The Upload page

Students and teachers of the project add photos, videos and texts through the Upload page. It is locked with a password. When someone uses it, the website keeps:

  • The name they type when they sign in. Only the website admin can see it. It is never shown on the public pages. A Journal story shows its author’s name only if the author chooses that.
  • A random access code, saved in their browser, so they do not have to type the password again. It is not a cookie and it is not used for tracking. “Log out” removes it.
  • Which uploads belong to them and when they last used the Upload page.
  • The IP address after a wrong password, to stop people from guessing passwords. It is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • The files and texts they upload, with the original file names. Photos are made smaller before they are sent, and this also removes their location data (GPS).

Everyone can edit or delete their own uploads. Deleted uploads stay in a trash folder for 7 days, then they are deleted for good. All of this is stored on Cloudflare’s servers.

Photos

The photos and videos on this website were taken by the students and teachers of the three schools during the project.

Are you in a photo and want it removed? Write to liceulhonterus@yahoo.com and we will take it down.
